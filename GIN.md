# Gin, is this even a real game?

## setup:

first round 2's are wild and each round the wild card goes up by 1 until Aces. Aces are high.

deal 7 cards each, flip a card onto the discard pile

next to dealer starts


## playing

on your turn you can either:

1) pickup the entire discard pile
2) pickup a mystery card

next if you haven't started putting cards down you can put down either:

1) 3 of the same card
2) 3 cards of the same suit in a run. i.e 5,6,7 or Q, K, A.

to finish your turn you discard a card.

## once you've put cards down:

once you have put 3+ cards down (you can't use any other cards on the table already to do this)
you can play at **anytime**. you can add cards to other peoples cards on the tables.

if there is a wild card on the table you can replace it with the card that would go in that position

i.e 5, 6, (wild), 8 of hearts, you could replace the wild for the 7 of hearts. you can pickup the wild
if you do this or play it right away.

there isn't crazy rearranging like rummy, just adding to existing sets and stealing of wild cards.

the round finishes when someone runs out of cards. you do not have to save a card to discard if you need
to use all your cards to go out.

## scoring:

once someone runs out of cards everyone else gets points based on what is in their hand.
number cards are worth the face value and picture cards are worth 10, wild cards are worth 15.

## winning:

whoever has the least points after the aces round wins!
